The battlefield card menu and the game's keyboard shortcuts now run the same card actions, and arrows and attachments go through one target pick, so the paths that had drifted apart now match desktop. "Draw arrow..." from a hand card plays the card and then draws the arrow from where it lands, as the right-button drag and desktop do, and an arrow to a card on the stack now targets that card on the stack rather than the battlefield. Every way of playing a card, and dragging a card onto the battlefield from another zone, now lands it with its printed power and toughness; a played card that comes into play tapped arrives tapped. While an arrow or attach target pick is pending, pressing on the board no longer starts a box selection, a real card drag cancels the pick, and Escape is left to an open dialog.
