## Menu as the long-lived API for PR 30

- **Names and props.** They are mostly consistent: `MenuItem`/`MenuRadioItem` take `onSelect`, `MenuCheckboxItem` takes `checked` + `onChange(checked)`, the entries share `disabled`/`disabledReason`/`shortcut`/`keyShortcuts`/`closeOnSelect`, and `closeOnSelect` defaults are right (item true, checkbox/radio false). Small rough edges, listed as nits:
  - `MenuSubmenu` takes its text as `label`, while the entries take `children`.
  - `MenuGroupProps` is not exported.
  - `placeMenu` and the three timing constants are exported from the public barrel only for specs.
- **Card menu: three nested submenus, disabled-with-reason, checkbox and radio items, shortcut hints.** All of it can be written without escape hatches. Tab, Escape and ← work at any depth (React-tree bubbling through the portals). `closeAll` reaches the root's `onClose` from any depth. → enters a hover-opened submenu. One cost remains: every entry needs two hand-written shortcut strings (finding below).
- **Flip-then-clamp at the four edges.** `placeMenu` is correct as a pure function:
  - Rect `below` flips up at the bottom.
  - Rect `right` flips left at the right edge, and up at the bottom.
  - Top and left only clamp.
  - Point anchors only slide.

  **In the browser the bottom edge does not work** (major below): the menu is measured while capped by its own provisional `maxHeight`. So the bottom-edge flip and clamp never trigger, for rect anchors and point anchors alike.
- **Hover delay / safe triangle.** This is a time-based "sloppy" window, not a geometric triangle, in the same spirit as QMenu. Accidental closes are avoided for diagonal paths under 300 ms, and resting on a sibling closes the submenu. No stuck-open case was found with the pointer. **With the keyboard,** a hover-opened submenu stays open after ↓/↑ moves to another entry (Probe D).
- **Type-ahead reset.** The buffer resets only on the 500 ms timer. It is not reset by arrows, Home/End, or the pointer, which is a nit. Space inside a run activates the focused item (major for game menus, below).

## Findings

| major | `src/components/Menu/Menu.tsx:147,156-160` | **Bottom-edge placement is broken in real browsers, and this regresses point anchors.** The first render uses `placeMenu(anchor, {0,0})`, whose `maxHeight` is `vh - top - 8`, i.e. just the room below the anchor. The layout effect then measures `getBoundingClientRect()`, and the panel's height is already capped by that `maxHeight` (`overflow-y-auto`). So the measured height always "fits below": a rect `below` anchor never flips above, a submenu never flips up, and a point anchor never slides up. A right-click or Shift+F10 near the bottom of the screen gives a few-pixel scroll box. `9e966a8` started from `maxHeight: vh - 16` and did slide (Probe A passes there and fails on the tip). Probe B, a 300 px menu on a control at y 700–720 in a 768 px viewport, stays at top 722 instead of 398. The specs miss it because their `getBoundingClientRect` mock ignores `style.maxHeight`. | Measure without the cap: render the first pass with no `maxHeight` (or `visibility: hidden` at 0,0), or measure `scrollHeight`, then apply the placed position and cap. Make the `Menu.spec` layout mock honour `style.maxHeight` (`Math.min(content, parseFloat(style.maxHeight))`). Add point and rect cases at the bottom edge. |
