## PR 12 — `parity/12-moderation-users` (diff vs `parity/03-protocol`)

Checked against desktop and found matching: menu gating, i.e. moderator section for IsModerator (admins carry it), per-role Demote-if-held / Promote-if-registered for IsAdmin only, everything disabled on self (`user_context_menu.cpp:421-480`). WarningDialog has the leading blank option and `%1 (IL %2)`. AdminNotes "Update Notes" is disabled until an edit. TabAdmin replay/activate messages match. TabLog defaults, the hours conversion and its messages also match.

## PR 04 — `parity/04-command-outcomes` (diff vs `parity/12-moderation-users`)

Verdict on the overall design: a synthesised `RespNotConnected` through the normal response path, with a typed third `onError` argument, follows desktop (`remote_client.cpp` `ping()` / `doDisconnectFromServer`) and is sound. The map is cleared before callbacks run, a late response is dropped, and the keepalive never sees a synthesised answer. The integration specs drive the real stack, with only the WebSocket mocked. The races left are at the edges below.

| major | `packages/sockatrice/src/WebClient.ts:117-120` (with `WebSocketService.ts:59-61`) | **Pending commands leak across `connect()`.** `connect()` on an open socket retires it with `closeActiveSocket(true)`, which detaches `onclose`.
