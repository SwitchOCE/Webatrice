### API gaps PR 30 will hit when it moves the live game menus onto `Menu`

1. **Anchoring is a point, not a rect.** `MenuAnchor` is `{x, y, align}`, and the clamp only slides. A card near the bottom of the board, or an opponent zone at the right edge, gets a menu pushed over the card it belongs to. `MenuSubmenu` anchors at `rect.right` and is slid back over its parent instead of flipping to `rect.left - width`. Add `anchor: { rect: DOMRect, placement: 'below' | 'right' }` with flip-then-clamp, and keep the point form for pointer opens.
2. **Nested submenus work, but hover takes focus and closes instantly** (minor finding above). Card menus have several submenus ("Move to", counters, P/T), so diagonal pointer travel will close them. There is also no keyboard path into a submenu opened by hover other than focus already being in it.
3. **Disabled items with reasons.** Items are native `disabled` (unfocusable, skipped) and the reason is only in `title`. Game menus have many state-dependent entries, and players need to hear why one is off. Add `aria-disabled` + `disabledReason` → `aria-describedby`.
4. **Shortcut hints.** `MenuItem.shortcut` is one string used both as the visible hint and as `aria-keyshortcuts`. ARIA needs the `Control+Shift+A` key-name syntax, with alternatives separated by spaces, while the visible hint should be the formatted, platform-aware keycap text. Split it into `shortcut` (display) and `keyShortcuts` (ARIA), or derive both from the shortcuts slice's sequence format. `MenuCheckboxItem` drops `shortcut` entirely (`Omit<…, 'shortcut'>`, :245), but game toggles such as reveal or face-down do carry shortcuts.
5. **No `MenuRadioItem`**, although `ITEM_SELECTOR` already includes `menuitemradio`.
6. **Closing is every caller's job** (`onSelect` doesn't close). Every converted game item would repeat `…; onClose()`. Provide `closeOnSelect` (default true for `MenuItem`) through `MenuLevelContext`.
7. **Type-ahead is single-character** (no buffer). This is fine for short menus, but game menus have repeated initials ("Move to …", "Mulligan").
