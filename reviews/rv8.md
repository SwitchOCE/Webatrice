Reviewed `origin/parity/05-refactor-seat` @ 0412500 against parent `origin/parity/06-e2e-hardening` @ d2e3d1e (53 commits, 173 files, +16468/−10543). Findings only; no code changed. Stage 5 (Phases 7–8) not reviewed.

- **Behaviour preservation is genuinely good.** Every `game.*` request with full payloads was recorded for all 34 PlayerBox characterization tests and the shared DnD tests at `8e21a19` (original PlayerBox) and at the tip: the traffic is identical except for the deliberate opponent-drag fix (4d56389).
